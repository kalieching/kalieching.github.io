about work media

Hi, I'm Kalie. I am extremely passionate about my work in tactile-aware manipulation policies for dexterous hands and loco-manipulation policies for humanoids at the Bosch Center for AI and Carnegie Mellon University.

Previously, I was a research engineer working on training reinforcement learning policies and visuomotor manipulation policies at the Robotics and AI Institute (formerly Boston Dynamics AI Institute) to teach robots how to perform new skills efficiently and safely. I have also worked on autonomous cars, robots on the International Space Station, and large-scale databases.

Other interests: fashion, character design, nutrition
